# Hearth HQ privacy notice — WORKING DRAFT (NOT PUBLIC)

**Do not publish or submit to Google Play until the legal controller identity, registration address, effective date, support handling, retention schedule, AI vendor contracts and data flows have been reviewed.** This draft is not a claim of compliance.

Hearth HQ provides household management features, including household profiles, bills and subscriptions, grocery lists, maintenance, document storage and scanning, optional health records, smart-home connections, an AI Chief of Staff, and optional permissioned banking integrations when approved for production.

## Information handled
- Account identifier, contact email and identity/household membership/roles.
- Household bills, expenses, tasks, groceries, maintenance and smart-home devices/status.
- Uploaded documents and scanned content, which may include highly sensitive personal information.
- Optional health records, medications and reminders.
- Optional financial institution connections, account metadata and transactions after explicit permission and provider approval; Sandbox simulations are not live banking.
- Usage and diagnostic activity needed to operate and protect the service; disclose exact analytics and device identifiers after SDK review.

## Purpose and access
Process information to provide the selected features, maintain accounts and permissions, show reminders/insights, protect the service and handle support. Data access depends on whether a record is private to the uploader or explicitly shared within the household. Bank balances and transactions remain owner-private under the current implementation. Never assume that creating a household grants blanket access to another member's private files or personal financial/health data.

## AI features
For AI-assisted requests, Hearth may send relevant user-authorized context to contracted model providers. Describe the providers, regions, retention/training policy and safeguards after verification. AI-generated answers may be inaccurate and should not be treated as medical diagnosis, regulated investment advice, or proof that an action or payment happened.

## Security and retention
Use encryption in transit, restricted database access and application-level encryption for Home Assistant long-lived tokens when rollout is complete. No promise that every data category is end-to-end encrypted. Publish a reviewed schedule distinguishing account data, files, logs, security records and legally required retention. Backup removal windows must be documented.

## Choices and rights
Users may request access, correction, export, revocation of provider consent or deletion via the signed-in Privacy screen or https://hearth-hq.onrender.com/account-deletion. Deletion is a **request**: verified processing is required and Google Play subscriptions must separately be cancelled in Play. Shared family records may have rights of other household members; legal retention exceptions must be explained. Contact: support@hearthhq.online (mailbox operation requires verification).

## International processing and third parties
Identify the company/controller, countries and contracts for Supabase, Render, EAS/Expo, RevenueCat, AI inference providers, banking and optional smart-home services before publication. Provide a current list of subprocessors and an appropriate cross-border transfer explanation.

## Children and updates
Set and enforce an approved minimum-user-age policy and guardian rules for dependant household information. State the effective date, revision notice method and privacy contact identity once finalized.
